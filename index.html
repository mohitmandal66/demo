The Heritage

Rooted in Assam.
Made for the moment.

At LIGANG, we honor the profound culinary heritage of Assam. Our approach strips away the unnecessary, leaving only the pure, bold flavors that define our ethnic roots within a contemporary, refined atmosphere.

Every ingredient is meticulously sourced from local artisans, and every technique respects the generations before us. This is an authentic dining experience presented with uncompromising elegance and spatial minimalism.
